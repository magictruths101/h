4. Reflection on Collaboration (500 words, written together). How did your group divide the labor?
Where did you disagree—about annotation choices, editorial framing, which images to include—and how
did you resolve those disagreements? The most interesting part of collaborative editorial work is often the
argument, and I’m interested to hear that. If there were no arguments, good for you; how did you
determine the direction it would take so peacefully? This brief reflection is the place to be honest about
that process.
